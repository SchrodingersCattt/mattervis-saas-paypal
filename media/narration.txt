Crystal structures often contain disordered atomic positions. Researchers need to compare possible ordered configurations without losing track of the original evidence.

MatterVis Studio keeps the original interactive viewer. Load a structure, rotate it, and choose how to explore the disorder. Here we enumerate alternative candidates and check the current scene before checkout.

This synthetic example has no hydrogen atoms. The source check makes that limitation visible before payment.

The researcher approves a two dollar PayPal Sandbox order. Once the server verifies the sandbox capture, the app generates candidate structures, checks each one, and asks an AI model to summarize the actual results and limitations.

Open any candidate in MatterVis or download the CIF files, checks, parameters, commands, and hashes together. These are hypotheses for human review, not proof of an experimentally dominant structure.
