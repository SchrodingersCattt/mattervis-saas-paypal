Crystal structures can contain disordered atomic positions. Researchers need usable models, clear checks, and control over the cost of advanced analysis.

MatterVis AI adds a conversation to the original interactive viewer. Ask it to inspect a structure or build a supercell, and it calls real scientific tools through the Model Context Protocol. The original structure remains available for comparison.

When the researcher requests disorder analysis, the paid MCP tool describes the method, candidate count, warnings, and price. It asks for explicit consent before creating a PayPal Sandbox order.

After the researcher agrees, they approve the virtual order in PayPal. The server verifies the capture, then generates and checks candidate structures. The assistant compares the evidence and explains its limitations.

Open each candidate in MatterVis or download the structures and provenance. No real money is charged, and the candidates remain hypotheses for human review.
